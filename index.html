GameSensitivity

Uses each game's yaw (degrees per mouse count): CS2 and Apex 0.022, VALORANT 0.07, Overwatch 2 0.0066. The result keeps your cm/360° the same. Values come from published conversion factors, verify in-game, and check your game's settings if a patch changes them.
